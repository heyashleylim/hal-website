PODCAST STUDIO
Prepares podcast episodes on your Mac: transcribes them, suggests chapters,
lets you check the chapters while listening, and exports the final MP3
(mono, 96 kbps, -16 LUFS, chapters inside). Everything stays on your Mac.


1. INSTALL (once)

   Open Claude Code and send this message:

     Unzip ~/Downloads/Podcast-Studio.zip into my home folder and set it up by following its CLAUDE.md

   Claude installs what's needed (it can take 5–10 minutes the first time).
   If Claude says Homebrew is missing, follow its instructions, then send the
   message again.


2. USE

   In Claude Code, send:

     Open Podcast Studio

   Your browser opens the Studio. Drop your MP3 files on the page and wait
   until each file says it's done transcribing.

   Then send:

     Suggest chapters for all files in Podcast Studio

   The chapters appear on the page by themselves. Click ▶ next to a chapter
   to listen from there, adjust the time or the title if needed.

   When they're right: tick the files and click "Export selected".
   The finished MP3s are in Downloads › Podcast Studio.
   Upload those to the podcast admin (Episodes page).


Without Claude Code: double-click "Podcast Studio.command" in the
Podcast-Studio folder, and use "Suggest from pauses" for chapters.
