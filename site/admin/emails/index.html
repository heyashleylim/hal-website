How the score works

Each email is compared with similar sends: the 30 broadcast emails closest to it in time whose audience was between half and double its size. So an email is judged against what was normal then, for an audience of that size.

  • Opens: how far its open rate is above or below the usual one, measured in “usual spreads”.
  • Clicks: the same for its click rate, only when the email had a link, and only against other emails with a link.
  • Score: the average of the two (opens only when there is no link). +2 or more is an outlier: about 1 email in 20 gets there by luck. +1 or more is above usual.

Not scored: sends to fewer than 300 people, emails younger than 3 days, and automation emails. The spreadsheet’s old “Perf” number is still shown when you open an email, so you can compare.
