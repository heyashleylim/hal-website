How this section works

  • Students’ words are never changed. Each text (sales call, onboarding questionnaire, written and video testimonial, each answer of a review) is kept exactly as it was written, with an English translation next to it. The database refuses any edit; a review sent again is kept as a newer version.
  • One profile per person. A student who took several programs (NSMB, then Life Artist) has one profile with a tab per program, linked by her ActiveCampaign contact or her email.
  • Snippets are always word for word. A snippet is a passage of a student’s text: the database checks every character. Quotes from the old analysis that were not word for word are kept in that analysis, marked “not word for word”, and are not snippets.
  • Reviews. Life Artist reviews come from the review form (saved here as soon as she sends it) or from an old export uploaded on the Import page.
  • Three analyses, your decision. The spreadsheet’s analysis (July 2026), an independent second read and a third read by another model (both November 2026) sit side by side. Where all of them agree, that is used; where they disagree, you choose.
  • Sensitive and private. Snippets about health, suicide, abuse or children are marked sensitive and left out of the snippet library by default. Mark on each student whether her words may be used publicly.
