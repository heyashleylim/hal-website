How this section works

  • Students’ words are never changed. Each text (sales call, onboarding questionnaire, written and video testimonial) is kept exactly as it was written, with an English translation next to it. The database refuses any edit.
  • Snippets are always word for word. A snippet is a passage of a student’s text: the database checks every character. Quotes from the old analysis that were not word for word are kept in that analysis, marked “not word for word”, and are not snippets.
  • Two analyses, your decision. The spreadsheet’s analysis (July 2026) and an independent second read (November 2026) sit side by side. Where they agree, that is used; where they disagree, you choose.
  • Sensitive and private. Snippets about health, suicide, abuse or children are marked sensitive and left out of the snippet library by default. Mark on each student whether her words may be used publicly.
