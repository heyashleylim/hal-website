How this works

  • Read-only on ActiveCampaign. The admin copies contacts from ActiveCampaign; it never creates, changes or deletes anything there.
  • Every day at 09:40 Seoul time it copies the contacts that are new or changed, and reads their tags, lists and fields. Once a week it goes through every contact.
  • Nothing is lost here. If someone deletes a contact inside ActiveCampaign, the admin keeps its copy and shows “no longer in ActiveCampaign” with the date.
  • Everything about a person in one place. Open a contact to see their tags and fields, their shop orders, their courses (Learning) and their student record (Students), matched by email or by the link made on the student’s page.
